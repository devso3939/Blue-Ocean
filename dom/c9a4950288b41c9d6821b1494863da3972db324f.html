We always try for our international patients feel as comfortable in Tbilisi, therefore, we offer the simplest and convenient option before starting treatment. It consists of three simple steps:

Use the online contact form of our website.

After you get in touch with us, we will answer all your questions and suggest you several treatment options depending on your individual needs and wishes. There will be always a web operator who will answer your enquiries 24/7.


After checking your enquiry, you will receive an answer by our dentists the very next day.

We will also send you an e-mail with your dentist(s) ‘detailed profile (education, qualifications, experience) as well as pictures of the clinic and the staff and real pictures of our patients before and after their treatment. We will discuss on where you want to stay and what kind of holiday you prefer.


Welcome to Georgia! Personal meeting at the airport and transfer to the hotel.

As soon as you land on the international airport of Tbilisi you will be met with our representative, bringing a sign board with our logo and your name neatly written. Then you will be transported to your hotel for accommodation

Better yet, see you in person.

We love our customers, so feel free to visit us during normal business hours.
